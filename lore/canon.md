# The Bounti Most Wanted: canon

This file is the source of truth for the villain series on @earnbounti. Everything posted must agree with it and with `state.json`. New facts invented in a post are added to `state.json` (per villain, under `lore_so_far`) the same day, so later posts never contradict them.

## The world

- **The Open Sea** is the internet where people earn. Honest hunters work it every day.
- **Curse Coins** are this world's cursed power. Bite one and you gain a single power, but you can never again earn an honest coin. Every villain made that trade, chose it, or was tricked into it. Only one person has ever held two (the Counterfeit King), which should be impossible.
- **Bounti HQ** issues bounties on the worst offenders. Bounties are measured in **AURA**, a reputation the whole sea understands. No AURA is ever paid out: it is fame, not money.
- **The hunters** are our followers. They capture villains by completing the BOUNTY REQUIRED task on a poster. They are never named or individually credited.
- Villains are always fictional. Never tie one to a real person, company, platform, country or group.

## Tiers (the order the world opens up, like One Piece's journey)

## How every arc is written (the One Piece way)

Before writing a new arc, read up on the villain's One Piece parallel (their arc, empire, backstory and motive). Use it to understand the shape of the story only. Never copy names, places, events, powers or lines from One Piece; translate everything into this world.

Each arc moves through these beats, one or more posts each:
1. **The empire first.** Show their reach and reputation before their face: what they rule, who fears them, a rumour.
2. **The reveal.** The poster drops with their name and bounty.
3. **The power.** What their Curse Coin does, shown through an incident.
4. **The backstory.** Why they became this. Some get a real tragedy that makes people feel for them; some are simply rotten.
5. **The hunt.** Sightings, near misses, escalating BOUNTY REQUIRED.
6. **The verdict.** CAPTURED (with how the hunters cornered them) or ESCAPED (bounty rises, they return later angrier).

## The roster

### Tier 1: The Small Fry

**Captain Barnaby "The Headline" Vane** (`barnaby-vane`). Parallel: Buggy.
- Empire: a fleet of loud, cheap ships that all scream at once. Whole harbours go deaf when he sails in.
- Coin: the **Echo Coin**. One shout becomes ten thousand. Every headline you could not scroll past was his.
- Backstory: a forgotten deckhand on a great crew who was never once noticed. He bit the coin so no one would ever ignore him again.
- Personality: coward, showman, hilarious, accidentally lucky. Keeps escaping and getting promoted by mistake; other villains start to think he is a mastermind. People love him.
- Weakness: silence. When nobody reacts, he shrinks.

**Silas Kroft, "The Butler"** (`silas-kroft`). Parallel: Captain Kuro.
- Empire: none on paper. He is the trusted admin of the biggest earning circle on the sea, the man everyone thanks.
- Coin: the **Vanish Coin**. Anything he wants gone (messages, records, himself) is gone.
- Backstory: tired of a life of serving, he spent three years collecting "membership fees" in silence, planning to disappear and live quietly.
- Personality: polite, patient, ice cold. Pushes his glasses up with one long nail before he strikes. People hate him.
- Weakness: his schedule. He cannot bear a plan going off-script.

**Morwenna Salt, "The Lure"** (`morwenna-salt`). Parallel: Alvida.
- Empire: a glittering pleasure ship where every door is an invitation.
- Coin: the **Lure Coin**. Anything she sends looks irresistible. Hunters who follow her links are never seen again.
- Backstory: once mocked for being plain, she bit the coin to never be overlooked. Vain, slippery, and secretly hungry to be truly admired.
- Personality: charming, theatrical, vain. Loved and hated in equal measure.
- Weakness: being ignored. Nothing slides off a hunter who does not click.

**Admiral Roderick Krieger, "Ten Thousand"** (`roderick-krieger`). Parallel: Don Krieg.
- Empire: the greatest armada on the Open Sea, ten thousand ships strong, all hollow.
- Coin: the **Legion Coin**. He splits into thousands of empty copies that follow, cheer and swarm on command.
- Backstory: he lost a real war on the far seas and came home with nothing but pride, so he built an army that can never desert him.
- Personality: brute, bully, cheat. Fakes surrender (begging, starving) and then strikes. People hate him.
- Weakness: one real fighter. His copies have no heart and scatter when one of them is tested.

### Tier 2: The Syndicate Captains

**Orrin Blackwater, "The Drowned Saw"** (`orrin-blackwater`). Parallel: Arlong.
- Empire: he rules a coastal town of creators and takes a cut of everything they make.
- Coin: the **Tide Coin**. He controls the currents, deciding whose work rises and whose sinks.
- Backstory: he grew up despised by the people on land and decided they would all work for him. Years ago he forced the town's most gifted young editor to make viral work for him, promising to free her town once she paid off a debt he never meant to let her finish.
- The editor (her name is revealed in his arc) is the heart of this arc and can later become an ally of the hunters.
- Personality: cruel, proud, loyal only to his own kind. Pure hate.
- Weakness: the truth spreading. When the town stops believing in the debt, his tide breaks.

**Don Cassius Vale, "Mr. Zero"** (`cassius-vale`). Parallel: Crocodile.
- Empire: a secret guild of numbered agents, run from a gambling palace in a desert kingdom where everyone believes he is a hero.
- Coin: the **Dust Coin**. Any wallet he touches turns to sand.
- Backstory: he once wanted to be the greatest on the sea and was humiliated by someone stronger. Now he wants a kingdom of his own, and he is drying out a whole country's earnings so it will beg him to rule.
- Personality: calm, contemptuous, brilliant. A villain people respect.
- Weakness: water. Hunters who keep earning honestly cannot be dried out.

**Dr. Halcyon Brine** (`halcyon-brine`). Parallel: Caesar Clown.
- Empire: a sealed laboratory island no one leaves.
- Coin: the **Fog Coin**. He conjures crowds out of smoke: fake buzz, fake fans, fake demand.
- Backstory: a brilliant scientist thrown out of the great lab for his experiments. He swore to prove that hype can be manufactured.
- Personality: cackling, cowardly, cruel to his own test subjects. Comic and despicable.
- Weakness: fresh air. One honest crowd blows his fog away.

### Tier 3: The Lords

**Saint Enoch Thane, "The Self-Made God"** (`enoch-thane`). Parallel: Enel.
- Empire: a floating island in the clouds where followers kneel and pay tithes for "secrets".
- Coin: the **Storm Coin**. He hears every message sent on his island and strikes anyone who doubts him.
- Backstory: born on a poor island, he decided that if no god would help him he would become one. Now he believes it.
- Personality: arrogant, bored, theatrical. Comic villain people love to see fall.
- Weakness: a hunter he cannot hear or frighten, someone grounded who does not need his secrets.

**Gideon Moor, "The Shadowkeeper"** (`gideon-moor`). Parallel: Gecko Moria.
- Empire: a vast ghost ship drifting in fog, crewed by shadows.
- Coin: the **Shade Coin**. He raises abandoned accounts and dead names into an obedient crew.
- Backstory: he once had a real crew he loved, and lost every one of them to a single betrayal by a stronger captain. He swore he would never lose a crew again, so he built one that cannot leave.
- Personality: lazy, grieving, theatrical. Tragic and sympathetic. People feel for him.
- Weakness: daylight. When the owners of those names wake up and come back, the shadows return to them.

**Lord Ferran Ashcourt, "The Puppeteer"** (`ferran-ashcourt`). Parallel: Donquixote Doflamingo.
- Empire: a beautiful kingdom of markets and festivals, where half the citizens are secretly on his strings.
- Coin: the **String Coin**. Invisible threads let him move anyone's account like a puppet.
- Backstory: born into the highest nobility, his family gave it all up and fell to the bottom, where a mob hunted them. He swore he would climb back and rule everyone who had hunted him. The iron collar he wears is from those days, and he keeps it on so he never forgets.
- Personality: charming, grinning, ruthless, magnetic. The love-to-hate villain.
- Weakness: one string cut in public. Once people see the threads, they all start pulling free.

### Tier 4: The Emperors

**Mother Marrow, "The Feast"** (`mother-marrow`). Parallel: Big Mom (Charlotte Linlin).
- Empire: a candlelit island of endless banquets, with dozens of sons and daughters ruling its halls.
- Coin: the **Soul Coin**. She takes years from people who sign her lifetime memberships and keeps them in her candles.
- Backstory: an abandoned child who only ever wanted one table where her whole family ate together. Everything she built is that table, and she will not let anyone leave it.
- Personality: warm, motherly, then terrifying in a heartbeat. Heartbreaking.
- Weakness: an empty chair. When her family starts to leave the table, her power gutters.

**Elias Thorne, "The Unbreakable"** (`elias-thorne`). Parallel: Kaido.
- Empire: a fortress country locked behind iron walls. No one in or out without his say.
- Coin: the **Iron Coin**. Nothing has ever broken him: no attack, no ban, no fall.
- Backstory: captured, chained and blindfolded young, he survived everything they did to him. He still wears the chains. Secretly he wants someone strong enough to finally stop him.
- Personality: brooding, drunk on his gourd, monstrous, almost sad.
- Weakness: unknown. His arc is about finding it.

### Tier 5: The Final Boss

**Bartholomew "Black Ledger" Crane, the Counterfeit King** (`bartholomew-crane`). Parallel: Marshall D. Teach (Blackbeard).
- Empire: no island of his own. He owns a piece of everyone else's.
- Coins: the only man to hold **two Curse Coins**: the **Ledger Coin** (rewrites what anything is worth) and the **Smoke Coin** (swallows other villains' power in black smoke).
- Backstory: patient, laughing, waiting for decades in other villains' crews, studying them, until the moment to take everything.
- He wears a fake crown, a knock-off of Bounti's own.
- Rule: he always ESCAPES, whatever the numbers say, until Mac decides his final arc. Every escape doubles his bounty.
- Personality: jolly, greedy, ambitious, horrifyingly smart.
